== The strategic shape

The programme is unusually cross-cutting: pure metaphysics at one end (WP-1, WP-5),
formal methods / programming-language theory in the middle (WP-2, WP-3, WP-6), and
interpretable / trustworthy AI at the other (WP-4). That breadth is a funding
_advantage_ if pitched to the right door and a _liability_ if pitched to the wrong one.
Three doors are worth most attention:

* *Leverhulme Trust* — because it explicitly rewards work that does not fit a
  disciplinary box. The single best fit for the programme as a whole.
* *John Templeton Foundation* — because its 2026 *Intelligence Venture* is asking, in as
  many words, what intelligence _is_ across human and artificial contexts. The
  trope-as-feature thesis (WP-4) speaks directly to it.
* *ARIA (Mathematics for Safe AI / Safeguarded AI)* — because it wants *mathematical
  proofs* about AI behaviour, which is what `trope-checker` is (WP-3).

== Funder detail (as of July 2026 — verify before use)

=== Leverhulme Trust — Research Project Grant
Up to *£500,000*, up to *5 years*, on a topic of the applicant's choice; open to
researchers at UK universities/HEIs. The Trust "does not work within a disciplinary
framework" and "particularly welcomes proposals that are not bound by conventional
disciplinary understandings"; assessment criteria are Originality, Importance,
Significance, and Merit. *2026 caveat:* during a move to a new grants portal, *outline
applications are paused between 27 February 2026 and September 2026*, and detailed
applications must be submitted by *01 September 2026*. Plan timing accordingly.
Source: https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/research-project-grants and the RPG-2026 change
notice.

*Framing.* Lead with the non-disciplinary angle: "a graded, computational dynamics for
the metaphysics of abstract particulars, with a formal checker and a neural-net
translation." This is the flagship application; align it to WP-1 + WP-4 + WP-5.

=== John Templeton Foundation — Intelligence Venture
Six funding areas (Character Virtue Development; Life Sciences; Public Engagement;
Individual Freedom & Free Markets; Mathematical & Physical Sciences; Religion, Science &
Society). From 2026 a cross-cutting *Intelligence Venture* (>$60M) asks whether there is
anything unique about human intelligence and whether intelligence is "written into the
fabric of reality." Applications begin with an *Online Funding Inquiry (OFI)*; a 2026
OFI deadline of *14 August 2026* was reported — verify. Templeton expects substantive
engagement with the sciences. Source: https://www.templeton.org/ (funding areas;
Intelligence Venture).

*Framing.* "What is a feature, and is it a universal or a particular?" — an ontology of
intelligence's building blocks, tested against real networks (WP-4). Keep the science
engagement front-and-centre; a purely metaphysical pitch will not clear their bar.

=== ARIA — Mathematics for Safe AI (Safeguarded AI)
ARIA is the UK's high-risk/high-reward funder (~£800M endowment). The *Mathematics for
Safe AI* opportunity space holds that "a combination of scientific world-models and
mathematical proofs may be the answer to ensuring AI provides transformational benefit
without harm"; the *Safeguarded AI* programme develops mathematical/scientific
approaches to trustworthy AI. Rolling *opportunity-seed* proposals (smaller exploratory
projects, reported up to ~£500k) are available. Source:
https://aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces and https://aria.org.uk/funding-opportunities.

*Framing.* The checker is a *proof-carrying loss verdict* for AI-processed
property-instances — a small, concrete instance of "mathematical proofs about AI
behaviour." Target a seed for WP-3 (operational model) + WP-2 (define O5).

=== AHRC responsive mode — standard research grant
Full economic cost between *£300k and £1.5M*, AHRC funds *80%* of FEC, up to *5 years*;
arts-and-humanities remit includes metaphysics. Source: UKRI/AHRC responsive-mode
listing (find-government-grants.service.gov.uk).

*Framing.* The pure-philosophy core (WP-1, WP-5): the dynamics of individuation-and-loss
as a contribution to the theory of abstract particulars.

=== British Academy / Leverhulme — Small Research Grant
Up to *£10,000*, up to *24 months*; open to UK-resident postdoctoral scholars, and
*independent scholars are eligible*; a recent round's independent-scholar deadline was
*27 May 2026* — verify the current round. Source:
https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/funding/schemes/ba-leverhulme-small-research-grants/ .

*Framing.* Seed money for the first paper (WP-1) and a pilot probe (WP-4). The fastest
route to a first funded output and a citable result.

=== Others worth a letter
* *EPSRC / UKRI* trustworthy & interpretable AI calls — for WP-3/WP-6/WP-7.
* *Open Philanthropy* and adjacent interpretability funders — for WP-4.
* *Learned societies* (Mind Association, Aristotelian Society, Royal Institute of
  Philosophy, British Philosophical Association) — small grants, workshops, travel;
  cheap ways to build the standing WP-8 needs.
* *ERC / MSCA* — subject to eligibility; large and cross-disciplinary-friendly.
* *Open University* internal research funds — the home institution; often the first and
  easiest pilot money.

== Prerequisites before any application (all in WP-8)

These are cheap and currently missing; every funder above will want at least the first
three:

. A *real ORCID* — the estate's `CITATION.cff` files carry the placeholder
  `0000-0000-0000-0000`.
. A *citable artefact with a DOI* — deposit a tagged release (e.g. Zenodo) so the
  workbench/checker can be cited in a bibliography.
. A *corrected citation record* — `docs/attribution/CITATIONS.adoc` and
  `CITATION.cff` currently carry an un-slugged project name (a space in the BibTeX key
  and URL). Fix before anyone cites them.
. A *one-page case for support* per target, drawn from the programme's WP descriptions.
. An honest *readiness statement* — the publication gate (`../../status/READINESS.adoc`)
  means implementation claims must be provisional until the tools reach grade B+.

== Honesty note

programme will be funded, nor that any figure or date here is current at the time of
reading. Treat every number as "last seen July 2026, verify." Amounts and deadlines are
the funders'; the fit judgements are the author's.
