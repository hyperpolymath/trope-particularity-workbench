== The big question (the Venture's own)

Is there anything unique about the representational substrate of intelligence? The
Intelligence Venture asks what intelligence *is* across human, animal, and artificial
minds. This project attacks one load-bearing corner of that question with unusual
precision: *what kind of thing is a learned feature?* Interpretability research treats
a model's features as **universals** — one repeatable property shared across all its
instances. A seventy-year-old tradition in metaphysics — trope theory (D. C. Williams
1953; Keith Campbell 1990) — says properties are **abstract particulars**: _this_
redness here, numerically distinct from _that_ exactly-resembling redness there. Which
ontology fits what neural networks actually contain is an open, empirically tractable,
philosophically foundational question — and nobody is working it: a targeted literature
sweep (July 2026) found no published work connecting trope theory to representation
learning.

== Why this is a Templeton project

* *Foundational, cross-field, and concrete.* It is analytic metaphysics with an
  experimental programme attached — exactly the Venture's cross-area shape (Mathematical
  & Physical Sciences × Philosophy & Theology × the science of intelligence).
* *It engages the sciences substantively.* The hypotheses are stated against the
  interpretability literature's own phenomena: superposition and polysemanticity map to
  the classic *piling* problem; the binding problem to *compresence*; feature-merging
  to the *exact-resemblance vs numerical-identity* distinction. Each mapping yields
  falsifiable probes (e.g. do SAE-separable feature-merges behave as labelled
  aggregation while inseparable ones produce the interference profile of silent
  conflation?).
* *It is already grounded in formal work.* The team maintains the first formal
  *dynamics* for trope theory — a machine-checked graded algebra of property-instance
  loss and deception (Lean 4 / Agda / Idris2) with a working verdict checker — so the
  philosophy is disciplined by proofs and the experiments by a vocabulary with exact
  semantics. (All tool-status claims provisional per the project's own publication
  gate: the checker is alpha; the claim funded here is the *research programme*, not a
  product.)

== Proposed work (2 years, sketch)

. *The metaphysics half.* Give the three unmapped classic problems of trope theory —
  swapping, piling, the resemblance-regress — their first graded, computational
  treatment, and defend the coordinate-exhaustiveness adequacy claim (is
  fate/bond/merge all the ways to individuate-and-lose?). Output: two papers for
  metaphysics venues.
. *The machine half.* Run the trope-vs-universal probes on open models: instance-level
  vs class-level feature representations; recoverable (labelled) vs irrecoverable
  (silent) merging under sparse-autoencoder separation; use-relative sufficiency of
  probed features. Output: one interpretability paper + open datasets/fixtures.
. *The synthesis.* A monograph-length essay for the Venture's audience: what the
  particularist ontology of features means for what machine intelligence *is* — and
  for what human abstraction is doing when it reads universals off a world of
  particulars.

== Outputs and outcomes

Papers (2 metaphysics, 1 interpretability, 1 synthesis essay), open formal artefacts
(proofs, conformance fixtures, probe datasets), and a workshop bridging the
metaphysics-of-properties and interpretability communities — two fields asking the
same question in mutual ignorance, which is precisely the kind of intellectual-humility
gap the Foundation exists to close.
