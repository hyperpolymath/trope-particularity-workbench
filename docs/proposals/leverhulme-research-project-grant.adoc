== The personal vision (Leverhulme's first criterion)

Trope theory — the metaphysics on which properties are particular, non-repeatable
instances — gave analytic philosophy a compelling *statics* of qualities and then
stalled, leaving its four classic problems (individuation, swapping, piling, the
resemblance-regress) contested for seventy years. Meanwhile we built machines whose
entire operation is the *dynamics* trope theory never wrote: systems that collapse,
detach, attenuate, merge, and misattribute billions of property-instances a second, with
no vocabulary for what is lost. This project completes trope theory — supplies the
missing dynamics with mathematical exactness — and uses the completed theory to answer
a question neither philosophy nor machine learning can answer alone: *what kind of
thing is a learned feature, and what does its particularity cost us when we abstract?*

== Originality / Importance / Significance / Merit (mapped to the Trust's criteria)

* *Originality.* The first dynamics for abstract particulars; the first machine-checked
  formalisation of any part of trope theory (a commutative graded algebra of loss and
  deception, proved in three independent proof assistants); the first bridge from the
  metaphysics-of-properties literature to neural-network interpretability (a July-2026
  sweep found no prior work connecting them).
* *Importance.* On the philosophy side: new formal traction on problems by which the
  theory has been judged since Williams (1953). On the machine side: a principled,
  proof-backed distinction between recoverable compression and silent conflation —
  the difference between an AI system that summarises honestly and one that is
  structurally wrong about how many things it is talking about.
* *Significance.* The framework travels: provenance, evidence law, scholarly editing,
  clinical records — anywhere the question "is what survived enough for this use?"
  matters. The verdict discipline (sufficiency relative to a declared use-model) is a
  general instrument.
* *Merit of design.* Philosophy disciplined by proofs; experiments disciplined by an
  exact vocabulary; every capability claim gated by the project's own published
  honesty regime (readiness grades, dated affirmation snapshots, no-overclaim rule).

== Programme of work (5 years, three strands + synthesis)

. *Strand A — The metaphysics (Years 1–3).* Graded, computational treatments of
  swapping (via the algebra's ratified commutativity), piling (labelled fusion vs
  silent conflation), and the resemblance-regress (via the provenance/record axis);
  the coordinate-exhaustiveness adequacy defence; a monograph.
. *Strand B — The formal core (Years 1–4).* Complete the operational-model
  mechanization (subject reduction through recursion; grade soundness for the
  conservative fragment); ratify and mechanize the veridicality-oracle interface;
  maintain the machine-checked core as the theory's arbiter.
. *Strand C — The empirical bridge (Years 2–5).* The trope-vs-universal probe
  programme on open models (instance-level features; recoverable vs irrecoverable
  merging under sparse-autoencoder separation; use-relative sufficiency of probes),
  with open datasets and fixtures.
. *Synthesis (Years 4–5).* The completed-theory statement: particularity as a graded,
  use-relative, mechanically checkable notion — and what that reframing does to the
  One-over-Many, to abstraction, and to trust in machine-mediated knowledge.

== Risks and honesty

The tooling beyond the checker is at design stage and is *not* what is being funded —
the grant funds theory, proofs, and probes, with the alpha checker as existing
infrastructure. Sources of risk: mechanization effort (mitigated: the hard algebraic
half is already proved), probe inconclusiveness (mitigated: either outcome — trope-like
or universal-like feature behaviour — is a publishable philosophical result).
