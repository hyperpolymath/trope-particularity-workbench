== One-paragraph pitch

Every AI pipeline transforms *property-instances* — a quality of _this_ patient, _this_
transaction, _this_ document — through summarisation, embedding, aggregation, and
paraphrase, with no formal account of what was lost, detached, or silently merged along
the way. We have built the beginnings of that account: a machine-checked *graded
algebra of loss and deception* (commutative monoid on a ratified carrier; proofs in
Lean 4 + Mathlib, Agda `--safe`, and Idris2 with no axioms behind claimed lemmas), a
language-neutral interchange format (Trope IR 0.2, frozen), and a conformance-green
checker that returns a decidable *sufficiency verdict with a witness edge* — which step
of the pipeline dropped below the declared floor. The seed would fund the step from
verified algebra to *usable safety instrument*: complete the operational-model
mechanization, bind the veridicality-oracle interface (O5), and demonstrate the checker
over a real ML data pipeline's provenance.

== Fit to Mathematics for Safe AI

ARIA's opportunity space holds that "a combination of scientific world-models and
mathematical proofs may be the answer to ensuring AI provides transformational benefit
without harm." This project is a small, concrete instance of exactly that claim:

* *Mathematical proofs, already machine-checked* — the grade algebra's cancellation
  boundary, effect-atom factorisation, and strict L4 monotonicity are sorry-free /
  axiom-clean Lean 4 results, with the metatheory in Agda and the executable core in
  Idris2 reusing the very algebra that is proved (no second implementation to drift).
* *A deployable trust boundary* — the checker consumes only the Trope IR (a JSON
  schema), so it bolts onto any pipeline that can emit provenance, independent of
  source language or framework.
* *Deception is structural, not statistical* — the three deceptive moves (false value,
  false bearer, false singleness) are absorbing bottoms in the algebra: they provably
  cannot compose away, and any honest floor rejects them. This is a mathematical
  guarantee about *information integrity through transformation*, complementary to
  model-behaviour safety work.

== What the seed buys (12 months, three work strands)

. *Mechanize the operational model.* The λ-trope small-step semantics with observed
  retention and the decidable `T-Fix-Decl` rule are designed (spec notes, 2026-07-07);
  the seed completes the Agda mechanization of subject reduction through `fix`
  (Theorem B Target 3) and grade soundness `g ⊑ ρ` for the conservative fragment —
  turning the designed model into a machine-checked one.
. *Bind the veridicality oracle (O5).* Formalise the oracle interface (soundness /
  attestation / no-laundering conditions) and implement its attestation shape as
  warrants in the store layer — the boundary between "provably composed honestly" and
  "checked against the world."
. *Demonstrate on a real pipeline.* Instrument one production-shaped ML data pipeline
  (candidate: a document-ingestion/embedding/RAG pipeline) to emit Trope IR; run the
  checker; show the witness edge localising real loss events; publish the conformance
  corpus extension.

Deliverables: machine-checked proofs (public), the O5 interface + reference
implementation, one instrumented demonstrator with a written case study, and two
papers (formal + systems).

== Honesty box (read first)

Per the estate's publication gate: the checker is *alpha* (conformance suite green,
10/10; verified core axiom-free); the front-end language and store are at design stage.
Nothing here claims a production system exists. The claim is narrower and checkable:
the *algebra* is proved, the *checker* runs, and the seed funds the distance from there
to a demonstrator. All capability statements are dated to the repos'
`AFFIRMATION.adoc` ground truth.
