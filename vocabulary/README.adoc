= Vocabulary — the nine effects
:toc: macro

One entry per effect: definition, the *discriminating test* against its nearest
neighbour (the part to carry — not the answers), and one worked example. The
examples are kept in lockstep with the checker's conformance fixtures
(`github.com/hyperpolymath/trope-checker`, `tests/conformance/`).

Two independent splits (see the README): *loss amount* (kept-whole vs lost-or-
changed) and *veridicality* (faithful vs the three deceptive duals
`p-falsifying` / `p-misbinding` / `p-conflating`).
