= `p-projecting`
:toc: macro

*Family:* effect  *·  Arity:* one  *·  Veridicality:* faithful

*Grade (calculus):* fate `Dropped` on the dropped fields, `Present` on the kept; bond `Withheld` if the bearer was dropped; merge `Single`.

== Definition

Named parts dropped on purpose. You know exactly which coordinates are gone — a reduced view, not a lie, provided nobody mistakes the projection for the whole.

== Discriminating test (vs `p-attenuating`)

Can you *name* exactly what is gone? Projecting drops a known, named field-set; attenuating loses an unknown amount with no clean structure.

== Worked example

A conference badge prints just your name, pronouns, and organisation; the form was built to keep those three and drop everything else. Named, intentional, fully-known loss.
