= `p-fusing`
:toc: macro

*Family:* effect  *·  Arity:* two  *·  Veridicality:* faithful

*Grade (calculus):* merge `Fused(τ)` with a required provenance tag τ; fates `Present`; bond the meet of the inputs'.

== Definition

Two instances deliberately blended into one *labelled* aggregate. Not an error — the plurality survives in the label, so downstream knows not to pull them apart.

== Discriminating test (vs `p-conflating`)

Same test as conflating, from the honest side: a fusion *carries its tag*. The tag is required — an untagged merge does not elaborate; it is a conflation fault.

== Worked example

A weather page shows today's regional figure as the mean of twelve station readings, labelled “average of 12 stations”. The label keeps the plurality visible. Against a use accepting a labelled merge: *p-sufficient*.
