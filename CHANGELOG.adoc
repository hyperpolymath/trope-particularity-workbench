==== Changed

  scaffolding (ABI/FFI seam, proof stubs, container and build trees, template
  docs, 19 template workflows) is removed. The authored vocabulary, trainer,
  map, examples, theory papers, whitepapers and proposals are unchanged.

  pipeline map beside it: https://hyperpolymath.github.io/trope-particularity-workbench/
