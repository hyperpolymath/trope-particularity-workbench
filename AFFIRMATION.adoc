A dated, ground-truthed honesty snapshot.

== As of 2026-06-22

*What this is:* the vocabulary — nine effects, the residual, the verdict — with a
discriminating test per effect and worked examples. It defines the words; it is not
the checker and not the language.

*Count, made consistent (EFFECT_COUNT=nine):* the seven original effects plus the two
deceptive duals `p-falsifying` (false value) and `p-misbinding` (false bearer); with
`p-conflating` (false singleness) these form the deceptive trio. `just check`
(`tests/check-vocabulary.py`) verifies the nine agree across `vocabulary/` (9 entries),
`assets/particularity-trainer.html` (9 options, 13 cases), `assets/trope-particularity-map.svg`
(three groups: no-loss / with-loss-faithful / deceptive), and the README arity
sentence ("seven single-instance, two two-instance").

*Lockstep:* the `examples/` (duck, record, person) track the checker's conformance
fixtures (`trope-checker/tests/conformance/`).

*Honest scope:* whether the three loss-coordinates are themselves exhaustive is an
open philosophy-of-individuation question (calculus O1-coord), logged not settled.
