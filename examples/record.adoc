= A record, laundered several ways
:toc: macro

A customer record — a property-instance about _this_ person's account — put through
the merge and projection moves. This is where the two-instance twins (`p-fusing` vs
`p-conflating`) and the projection/loss moves show their seams.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Move | What happens | Why it is that move
| `p-projecting` | An API returns only id, display name, avatar — exactly the fields the client asked for; the rest stays on the server, deliberately unsent. | Named, intentional, fully-known loss. You can say precisely what is gone. (`badge-project.ir.json`)
| `p-fusing` | A regional figure shown as the mean of twelve station readings, labelled “average of 12 stations”. | Many blended into one, openly labelled — plurality recoverable from the label. (`weather-fuse.ir.json`)
| `p-conflating` | Two customers both named Alex Doe; support merges their histories into one record and replies as one person, with nothing noting the merge. | Two asserted to be one, unlabelled — downstream is silently wrong. Deceptive: a false singleness.
|===

The discriminating test runs straight down the table: projecting tells you *what* is
gone; fusing keeps the plurality in a *label*; conflating discards the label and so
lies about how many there were.
