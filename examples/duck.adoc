= The duck, laundered several ways
:toc: macro

One property-instance — _this_ duck's hoarse morning call, on _this_ pond, today —
put through different effects. Each row names the move and the verdict against a
floor demanding the call's spectral content. The IR for these is in the checker's
conformance suite (`trope-checker/tests/conformance/fixtures/`).

[cols="1,2,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Move | What happens to the call | Verdict (spectral floor) | Checker fixture
| `p-preserving` | Lossless audio + ring number, pond, date, weather. | `p-sufficient` | `duck-preserve.ir.json`
| `p-collapsing` | Logged as one column: heard a quack? → yes. | `p-insufficient` (witness: the collapse) | `duck-collapse.ir.json`
| `p-attenuating` | Saved as a heavily compressed clip; an unknown amount of detail blurred. | `p-insufficient` (⊤ below every finite fidelity) | `letter-attenuate.ir.json` (same shape)
| `p-detaching` | The call clipped and shared with no bird, no pond, no date. | `p-insufficient` if the use needs the bearer | `interview-detach.ir.json` (same shape)
|===

The point: *nothing about the call changed across the verdicts* — only the use-model.
The same collapse that is insufficient for diagnosing the bird is sufficient for
merely counting that _a_ duck was heard.
