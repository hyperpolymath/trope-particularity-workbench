= A person, laundered several ways
:toc: macro

A quotation — a property-instance: _this_ thing said by _this_ person, in _this_
context. The person case is where the *veridicality* axis shows itself: the
difference between honest absence and deceptive falsehood.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Move | What happens | Axis
| `p-detaching` | A vivid line lifted from an interview and shared alone — no speaker, no question, no context. | *Loss* (honest): the bearer is genuinely absent; the bond is `Severed`. Nobody is told a false speaker.
| `p-misbinding` | The same line published with a named speaker attached, confidently — but said by someone else. | *Veridicality* (deceptive): the bond is asserted intact, to the *wrong* bearer. A false bearer, not a missing one.
| `p-falsifying` | The line itself altered and presented as a verbatim quote. | *Veridicality* (deceptive): a *false value* dressed as faithful. Substitution, not loss.
|===

`p-detaching` vs `p-misbinding` is the clean test for the bond axis: *absent* (honest)
versus *wrong* (deceptive). Under the checker's prevent profile the two deceptive
moves are not even writable — an emitted `Misbound` or `Falsified` grade is a fault
caught at the validation boundary, with the offending coordinate named.
